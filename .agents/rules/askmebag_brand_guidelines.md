# ASKMEBAG Brand & Design Guidelines

## 1. Core Creative Principle
- Do NOT use the usual AI-generated definition of premium (no excessive glassmorphism, no gradient blobs, no floating geometric decorations, no generic luxury photography).
- Banned buzzwords: "Elevate your...", "Unlock...", "Seamless...", "Transform...", "Revolutionary...".
- The website must feel designed by a human art director who understands real manufacturing.

## 2. Anti-AI-Slop Rule
- For every section, ask: "Could this exact section appear on 500 other AI-generated websites?"
- If YES: DO NOT ship it. Change composition, visual tension, typography hierarchy, image treatment, or copy rhythm.
- Every unconventional choice must have a reason.

## 3. Visual Language & Layout
- Use editorial composition, industrial photography, material textures, intentional asymmetry, strong typography hierarchy.
- Restrained color: Deep charcoal, warm ivory, muted earthy accents.
- Layouts must have deliberate visual tension (asymmetric grids, overlapping imagery, edge-aligned content). 
- Do not make every section centered or the same card grid. Change visual rhythm between sections.

## 4. Copy Principles
- Write like one knowledgeable person speaking to another person. Avoid marketing language.
- Use specific language (fabric, stitching, dimensions, quantity).
- Do NOT invent years in business, customer numbers, client names, or testimonials. Use placeholders if needed.
- Human imperfection: Vary sentence length. Use fragments, technical labels, and deliberate pauses.

## 5. Image Principles
- Prefer real manufacturing environments: sewing machines, fabric rolls, cutting tables, hands working, stitching details.
- Avoid generic office stock photography or meaningless lifestyle images.
- Photography must have believable light, texture, and physical imperfections.

## 6. Motion Principles
- Motion should reveal information (image masks, subtle parallax, typography reveals).
- Avoid constant floating, bouncing, or excessive blur. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 7. Design Review Checklist (MUST PASS BEFORE DELIVERY)
1. Does this look like an AI-generated website? (Must be NO)
2. Could this appear on 500 SaaS websites? (Must be NO)
3. Is the layout too symmetrical? (Must be NO)
4. Is there unnecessary whitespace/decoration? (Must be NO)
5. Is the photography believable? (Must be YES)
6. Is there a deliberate visual tension point? (Must be YES)
7. Does it look different from the previous section? (Must be YES)
8. Would a human designer actually keep this? (Must be YES)
